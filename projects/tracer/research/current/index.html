Your data

Your videos. Your Dropbox. Your control.

Most screen recorders host your videos on their servers — and quietly own the relationship with your viewers. Tracer flips that around.

The recorder uploads each file to your Dropbox account. The shareable page just streams from there. If you ever leave Tracer, nothing breaks: your videos are sitting in ~/Dropbox/Tracer/ exactly where you left them.

  • Zero video storage on our side. We host metadata only.
  • No watermarks, ever. Even on the free tier — because there’s only the free tier.
  • Revoke at any time. Disconnect Dropbox and your videos stop being public.
  • Open source. Don’t trust us — read the code.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?
Yes. Tracer is open-source and free forever. There is no paid tier, no premium add-ons, and no “starting at $0/month” trick. It's built and maintained by NoCorny Agency as a public good.
Why Dropbox specifically?
Dropbox is the simplest way to give every user real, portable file ownership without us building a storage backend. Your recordings live in a folder you already trust — and you can open, delete, or move them outside of Tracer at any time. Other providers may come later.
What if I disconnect Dropbox?
Existing share links stop streaming (since the file isn't accessible anymore), but your video files are untouched in your Dropbox. Reconnect and everything works again.
Do my viewers need an account?
No. Viewers click the link and watch. There's no sign-up wall, no email gate, and no paywall in front of your content.
What about Windows or Linux?
The recorder is macOS-only today. The web app is cross-platform — anyone can sign in to manage videos, but recording is Mac-first for now. Contributions on GitHub are very welcome.
How does the AI titling work?
Once your file finishes uploading, Tracer transcribes the audio and uses a small language model to generate a title and a one-paragraph description. You can always edit them — and you can disable AI features in settings.
Is this affiliated with Loom?
No. Tracer is an independent open-source project from NoCorny Agency. We just like the workflow Loom popularized — but think it should be free, transparent, and yours.
